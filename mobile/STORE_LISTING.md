### Review notes
RacketBuddy lets users create an account directly in the app. A reviewer can choose “Ny her? Opret profil” on the login screen and create a profile without an invitation code.

Court and coach payments are for real-world physical services. The app may open RacketBuddy's secure web checkout to complete those payments.

Account deletion is available inside the app under “Min profil” → “Slet konto permanent”.

- App access: all features available after free sign-up; no invite code. Reviewers can sign up on the login screen.
