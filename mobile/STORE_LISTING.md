### Review notes
RacketBuddy lets users create an account directly in the app. A reviewer can choose “Ny her? Opret profil” on the login screen and create a profile without an invitation code.

Court and coach payments are for real-world physical services. The app may open RacketBuddy's secure web checkout to complete those payments.

Account deletion is available inside the app under “Min profil” → “Slet konto permanent”.

## Store data checklist

Verify these answers against the production configuration immediately before submission:

- Account information: name and email are collected to create and operate an account.
- User-generated content: match posts and messages may be processed to provide the service.
- App activity: bookings and match activity are processed to provide the service.
- Approximate location: the app currently asks for a user-selected region/area; it does not request GPS permission in the mobile code.
- Payment card details: not collected by the mobile app; checkout is handled externally by the payment provider.
- Advertising/tracking: no advertising or cross-app tracking SDK is currently included in the mobile project.
- Account deletion is available in-app and at the public deletion URL above.
